Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

This policy explains how RLPSCOUT AI LLC (“RLPSCOUT”, “we”) handles personal information through this website. The RLPScout software application has its own Privacy Policy, shown inside the application.

1. What this site collects

Very little. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising trackers and has no accounts.

  • Inquiries. The inquiry form does not send anything to us by itself. It opens your own email app with a message you can edit. If you send it, we receive what you choose to include, such as your name, organization, email address and details of a requirement or building.
  • Emails you send us. The same applies when you write to any address listed on this site.
  • Technical data. Like any website, our host and font provider receive your IP address and browser details when you load a page (see section 3).

2. How we use it

We use what you send us to reply, to evaluate a requirement, building or partnership you propose, and to keep records of our business correspondence. We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

3. Service providers

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site and may log visitors’ IP addresses for security and operations.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces. Your browser requests them directly from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • Our email provider stores the messages you send us.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a sale of our business.

4. How long we keep it

We keep correspondence as long as it is useful for the purpose it was sent and for as long as business, legal and contract records must be kept.

5. Your choices and rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it, subject to records we must keep. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under state law. We will not treat you differently for using them.

6. Children

This site is for businesses and government officials and is not meant for children.

7. Changes

We will post any change here with a new date.
