# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences with equal weight:

- **Government leasing and contracting officers** (GSA Public Buildings Service, agencies with their own leasing authority, state and local space requirements). They are evaluating whether RLPSCOUT AI LLC is a credible, registered offeror for a Request for Lease Proposals: registration, process, and how offers are checked against the requirement.
- **Building owners and strategic partners** (owners, developers, build-out partners). They are deciding whether to give RLPSCOUT site control or partner capacity so their building can be offered against federal requirements.

Licensed real estate professionals in new states are a smaller, secondary audience.

## Product Purpose

The company website of RLPSCOUT AI LLC. It explains that the firm responds to government lease requirements in its own name, as offeror and lessor, and earns an inquiry: a requirement or RLP from an officer, a building from an owner, or a partnership.

## Positioning

One accountable party from the RLP to the lease. RLPSCOUT reads every line of the RLP, traces each requirement to its source (document, page, quote), checks it against the building before offering, and signs as lessor of record. Technology does the reading; partners and licensed professionals make the commitments.

The offeror and lessor positioning is confirmed for this website (2026-11-20). It is separate from the RLPScout app, which is a neutral tool and does not bid.

## Operating Context

Officers read RLPs, SAM.gov registrations, and capability statements; they compare offers on technical acceptability, timeliness, and price per ABOA square foot. Owners think in terms of site control, build-out, and lease terms.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site: `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`, `js/hero.js` (canvas hero animation), `assets/mark.svg`. No build step.
- The contact form opens the visitor's email app addressed to George Mogga; there is no form backend.
- The capability statement is an in-page dialog (`#cap-dialog`) with a print-to-PDF path; `#capability-statement` in the URL opens it.
- Copy may be tightened for clarity, but every fact, number, name, registration ID, and claim must be kept.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: RLPSCOUT (wordmark), legal name RLPSCOUT AI LLC.
- Voice: plain, factual, government-procurement literate, candid ("New firm. No federal awards yet.").
- Mark: `assets/mark.svg` and the inline bracket mark in the header.

## Product Principles

1. Credibility comes from verifiable facts (registration, process, sources), never from claims the firm cannot back.
2. Show the work: a requirement traced to its source is the strongest proof the firm has.
3. Serve both audiences without making either feel secondary.
4. Candor over polish: state what the firm does not have yet.
