What we do

One accountable party, from the RLP to the lease.

We do not broker space to the government and step away. We respond in our own name, secure the building, and sign as lessor. Each offer follows the same four steps.

Step 1of 4

We are the offeror.

RLPScout submits the offer and signs the lease as lessor of record. We secure site control through agreements with building owners and strategic partners, so the government deals with one party that answers for the space, the schedule, and the price.

  • Offer submitted in RLPSCOUT AI LLC's name, registered in SAM.gov
  • Site control documented before the offer goes in
  • One point of contact through award, build-out, and acceptance
Step 2of 4

We search inside the delineated area.

The delineated area in the RLP is a hard boundary, so we treat it as one. We match the boundary against more than 5,000 tracked buildings, then work with our licensed real estate professional in that state to confirm availability, ownership, and the owner's willingness to commit.

  • Candidates plotted against the exact boundary streets
  • Buildings outside the boundary excluded, not argued for
  • Local market checked by a licensed professional on the ground
Step 3of 4

We check every line against the building.

We parse the RLP and its attachments into individual requirements: ABOA square footage, parking, security, floodplain, accessibility, fire and life safety, and the delivery schedule. Every requirement is traced to its source, by document, page, and quote, and marked as met, not met, or needing work before we offer.

  • Each requirement linked to the exact page and wording
  • Gaps found before submission, not after award
  • A reviewer can follow every decision back to the RLP
Step 4of 4

We submit a complete package before the deadline.

An incomplete offer is a weak offer. We assemble everything the RLP asks for, check it against the submission list, and deliver it before the due date and time.

  • GSA Form 1364 Proposal to Lease Space
  • GSA Form 1217 Lessor's Annual Cost Statement
  • GSA Form 3518-SAM Representations and Certifications
  • GSA Form 12000 Prelease Fire Protection and Life Safety Evaluation
  • Floor plans Showing the offered space and ABOA calculation
  • Site control Evidence of the right to offer the building
  • FEMA flood map Floodplain status for the site

Strategic partners

Licensed professionals

Commitments

Three commitments, measured the way the government measures them.

  1. Right space

    Every line of the requirement is checked against the building before it is offered. If the building does not meet the RLP, we do not offer it.

    Evaluated as Technically acceptable on every stated requirement

  2. On time

    Every offer is complete and in before the deadline. The space is ready by the date in the lease, with build-out planned backward from occupancy.

    Evaluated as Offer received by the due date, space accepted by the lease date

  3. Fair price

    Priced to compete, with the numbers laid out the way the government compares them: shell rent, operating costs, tenant improvements, and amortization, per ABOA square foot.

    Evaluated as Price per ABOA square foot, compared across offers
